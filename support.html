IoM Theory

Support

IoM Theory is a study app for the Isle of Man driving theory test, for car and motorcycle learners.

Common questions

  • How do I switch between car and motorcycle? Profile tab → Study mode (Car/Motorcycle). Motorcycle mode adds rider lessons and mixes rider questions into every exam.
  • How do I reset my progress? Profile tab → Reset all progress. This only affects your device — the app stores nothing anywhere else.
  • Are the mock exams like the real test? Yes — the Full Mock uses the real format: 50 questions in 57 minutes, with a pass mark of 43.
  • Does the app cover the hazard perception part of the test? No — it covers the 50 multiple-choice questions. The real test also has 14 hazard perception clips, taken at the same sitting, so practise those with the official materials.
  • Is this an official Isle of Man Government app? No. It is an independent study aid based on the Isle of Man Highway Code and public guidance. Rules change — always check gov.im before your test.

Report a problem or a content error

Spotted a wrong answer, a typo, or a bug? Please open an issue here with the question text or a screenshot. Content corrections are the highest priority and ship in the next update.
